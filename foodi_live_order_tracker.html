Product proposal

Why this feature matters

Customers should never have to guess what’s happening after checkout.

🛡️
Builds customer trust

Clear status and delivery estimates reduce uncertainty.

🔔
Proactive updates

Show useful changes at confirmation, preparation, pickup, and delivery.

🎧
Fewer support questions

Make “Where is my order?” easier to answer without contacting support.

⭐
Better customer experience

A more predictable delivery journey can encourage repeat orders.

Why approve development?

Start with a small MVP: order status timeline, ETA, and notifications. Validate with a pilot before expanding to live rider-map tracking.

Prototype note: All order, rider, time, and map details are fictional sample data. The buttons simulate states; no real order or delivery service is connected.
